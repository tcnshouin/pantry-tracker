## 1. Hunter — Novice Tier (E–D rank)
Young male hunter, late teens, athletic lean build, average height, not bulky.
**Face:** sharp angular jaw, slightly pointed nose, thin eyebrows, dark determined eyes.
**Hair:** black, spiky, irregular, medium length, spikes pointing upward/outward, a few loose strands framing the face, no shine streak.
**Clothing:** dark charcoal-grey hooded jacket (hood down, resting on shoulders), zip partially open at collar; dark grey crew-neck shirt underneath; small worn patch on left shoulder; dark slim-fit pants tapered at ankle; worn dark leather boots with simple lacing. No armor, no glow, no accessories — civilian, rough, budget look.
**Hands:** right hand = closed weapon-grip fist (empty, no weapon — Option B); left hand relaxed/open.
**Posture:** slight natural slouch, a bit unsure of himself.
**Faces:** RIGHT.

## 2. Hunter — Hunter Tier (C–A rank)
Same character as Novice — IDENTICAL face (sharp angular jaw, dark eyes) and IDENTICAL black spiky irregular hair with loose strands. Same body proportions, but leveled up.
**Clothing:** fitted blue-and-black combat jacket, structured/form-fitting, light matte armor shoulder plating on both shoulders and upper chest, collar stands up slightly; dark navy crew-neck compression shirt underneath; dark tactical pants with subtle side-seam detail; black combat boots with reinforced toe caps and ankle buckle; dark fingerless tactical gloves on both hands.
**Hands:** right hand = closed weapon-grip fist (empty, fingerless glove visible); left hand relaxed open, glove visible.
**Posture:** confident — back straight, shoulders back/squared, calm assured expression, no slouch.
**Special:** faint cyan edge-light/rim highlight along right shoulder and arm (subtle, not a glow).
**Faces:** RIGHT.

## 3. Hunter — Monarch Tier (S+ rank)
Same character — IDENTICAL face and IDENTICAL black spiky hair. Maximum power form.
**Clothing:** long black armored coat to mid-thigh, structured with overlapping dark plate segments on chest/shoulders/upper arms, cyan-violet glowing energy trim along all edges; black high-collar bodysuit underneath; dark armored gauntlets (full coverage, not fingerless) on both hands; tall black armored combat boots with slight heel.
**Aura:** faint violet-cyan aura — thin energy wisps drifting upward from coat hem and shoulders.
**Eyes:** normal dark eyes with a very faint violet glow in the iris (subtle).
**Hands:** right hand = closed weapon-grip fist, armored gauntlet, empty (no weapon), faint energy crackling around knuckles; left hand relaxed, gauntlet visible.
**Posture:** commanding — feet slightly wider than shoulder-width, back perfectly straight, chin slightly raised, regal/imposing, expression cold and composed.
**Faces:** RIGHT.

## 4. Goblin
Small goblin, roughly 60% the height of a human, wiry scrappy build, limbs slightly too long for the body, large head relative to body.
**Face:** wide flat nose, beady round orange eyes set close together, big jagged lower teeth in a permanent sneer, large pointy ears sticking outward. Medium saturated green skin, slightly mottled. No hair.
**Clothing:** ragged dark brown loincloth held by a frayed rope belt; bare feet with short clawed toes.
**Weapon:** rusty iron dagger in the right hand — short blade, crude wooden handle wrapped in worn leather strips, visibly corroded/pitted.
**Posture:** classic goblin hunch — back rounded forward, knees bent and splayed outward, weight on balls of feet, head pushed forward, wary darting eyes.
**Faces:** LEFT.

## 5. Imp
Tiny flying imp demon, even shorter than the goblin. Very thin and bony — elongated limbs, narrow torso. Hovers ~20% of its body height above ground.
**Face:** small round head, two small curved horns on top pointing slightly outward, large bright yellow glowing eyes filling most of the socket, tiny pointed nose, wide thin mouth in a sinister grin with small sharp teeth. Muted purple-violet skin throughout.
**Body:** thin angular torso, long thin arms ending in three-fingered clawed hands, long thin legs ending in two-toed clawed feet. Two large leathery bat wings from upper back (~1.5x body width), semi-translucent purple at tips, deep purple at base. Small pointed tail curling behind. No clothing.
**Faces:** LEFT.

## 6. Shadow Wolf
Four-legged shadow wolf, large predatory beast — body roughly as wide as a crouched human, low-slung powerful build.
**Fur:** dark grey-black, shaggy, slightly matted/uneven; longer fur around neck/chest forming a rough mane; thick tail held stiffly behind.
**Face:** large lupine head, elongated snout, bared upper/lower fangs, icy blue glowing eyes (narrow, predatory), black nose, pointed ears flattened back, slight scarring across muzzle.
**Body:** thick muscular legs, large paws with visible dark claws on all four feet; front legs slightly longer than hind legs giving a forward lean.
**Posture:** predatory low stance — all four paws planted, spine roughly parallel to ground, weight slightly forward, head lowered to shoulder level — active hunting crouch, not sitting.
**Faces:** LEFT.

## 7. Orc
Large orc brute, significantly taller and wider than the hunter — massively muscled, barrel chest, thick neck, heavy limbs. Slow but devastating.
**Skin:** green-grey, slightly mottled, visible muscle definition.
**Face:** broad flat face, wide flat nose, small deep-set red eyes, prominent lower jaw with two thick tusks curving upward from lower lip, heavy brow ridge, bald head with a few scars.
**Clothing:** scrap metal shoulder armor — mismatched plates bolted to leather straps on both shoulders, chest/arms bare; ragged dark brown pants cut off at the knee, thick leather belt with crude iron buckle; bare feet with thick clawed toenails. No shirt.
**Weapon:** large heavy iron club-maul — thick cylindrical handle, large flat heavy iron head, visibly dented, held in both hands resting on right shoulder.
**Posture:** wide threatening neutral — feet wider than shoulder-width, toes out, knees slightly bent, weight low/stable, contemptuous sneer showing tusks.
**Faces:** LEFT.

## 8. Gatekeeper (Boss — 384×384)
Massive armored demon warrior — towering, nearly twice human height, powerfully built, extremely broad shoulders.
**Skin:** dark crimson-red, visible on face and exposed hands.
**Armor:** full heavy plate armor over the entire body — dark gunmetal grey with red-brown rust staining at seams; large angular chest plate design; wide jutting shoulder plates with short downward spikes; greaves on shins; gauntlets on both hands. Ancient and battle-worn — dented, scratched, chipped edges.
**Helmet:** large full-face visor helmet with two curved ram-style horns curving upward from the sides; narrow horizontal visor slit glowing orange-red — eyes not fully visible, just a menacing glow.
**Weapon:** massive two-handed greatsword — blade as long as a human is tall, wide thick dark steel, angular crossguard, held in right hand with tip resting on ground.
**Posture:** imposing neutral stand — feet shoulder-width, back straight, full height, completely still, radiating menace.
**Faces:** LEFT.

## 9. Stone Golem (Boss — 384×384)
Colossal humanoid of living rock — the largest enemy in the game, extremely wide and massive.
**Material:** entire body is grey cracked rock, cracks filled with dark green moss and amber-tinged dirt; rough/textured surface, not smooth; large/small rock chunks form arms, torso, legs. No clothing, no armor.
**Core:** perfectly circular glowing cyan-blue energy core embedded in center of chest — smooth, bright, pulsing — the only luminous element on the body.
**Face:** two recessed eye sockets with tiny cyan glowing pinpoints deep inside; no nose, no mouth; head is a large irregular boulder sitting directly on shoulders with no neck.
**Arms/Legs:** massive arms (each as wide as a human torso), blocky forearms, huge cuboid fists; thick rock-pillar legs with flat rock-platform feet.
**Posture:** dormant stand — both feet flat, very wide stance, both massive fists hanging at sides, core glowing at rest, head angled slightly downward — sense of enormous weight/gravity.
**Faces:** LEFT.

## 10. Shadow Soldier (Ally)
Raised undead knight made of living dark energy.
**Material:** entire body is dense black-and-deep-violet shadow matter — semi-transparent at edges, wisps of dark energy drifting upward from shoulders, hem, and feet. Humanoid soldier silhouette but no physical substance — darkness given form.
**Face:** no facial features visible inside the hood — complete darkness within, except two small bright cyan pinpoints of light where eyes would be. Cold, sharp, faintly glowing.
**Form:** reads as a humanoid soldier in light armor and a hooded cloak, but all shapes are shadow matter (not real fabric/metal); hood covers head; shoulder plates implied by shape.
**Weapon:** spectral dark sword in right hand — blade of compressed shadow energy with faint violet-cyan edge, no physical material. Left hand open, no weapon.
**Posture:** at-attention standing — feet together, sword pointing straight down at right side, left arm relaxed, eerie stillness, shadow wisps drifting upward.
**Faces:** RIGHT.

## Shared rules across all characters
- Transparent PNG background (opaque only for the stage background).
- Canvas: 256×256 for regular characters/hunter/shadow, 384×384 for bosses, 96×128 for weapons, 1280×800 for background.
- Camera: eye-level, slight 3/4 turn (70% side profile / 30% toward camera), full body head-to-feet, flat orthographic — no perspective distortion.
- Style: clean 2D anime mobile-RPG cel-shading, 2–3 shading tones, rim light from upper-left, dark (not pure black) outlines.
- Hunter & Shadow Soldier face RIGHT. All enemies/bosses face LEFT.
- Hunter's right hand is always an empty weapon-grip fist (Option B — weapons are separate overlay sprites).
